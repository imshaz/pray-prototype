Delegated access

Let someone help run the ministry — without handing them the keys to everything.

Today, admin access is all-or-nothing. There is one Admin role, and it can do everything — every user, every setting, the whole community. So asking someone to simply help work prayer requests currently means handing them power to manage every person in Charleston. Delegated access fixes that: you hand over a defined slice of the work, and nothing more. This is a platform capability (X-27, X-30) — it belongs to Thriving Communities as a whole and works the same for any area of the product. It's being built for the prayer areas first.
Who has access
4 people · Charleston
You can always see who can touch what (X-21). Every action a delegated admin takes is recorded against them by name — never as "an admin" (X-24). And revoking access takes effect at once, not at their next login (X-22).

Notice there's no "manage users" or "billing" area to hand out — a delegated admin never touches the whole community, only the slices you choose (X-23).
Where this is headed. Because delegated access is a platform capability, the same six-area model will extend to other parts of Thriving Communities beyond prayer. Reviewing it here, in the prayer ministry, is the first place it appears.

Give someone access

Pick a person in your community, then choose the areas of work they can help with. Everything outside those areas stays off-limits.

Only people already part of the Charleston community can be chosen.
Charleston only fixed — cannot be platform-wide
Access is always scoped to this one community. No delegated admin can ever act across other communities.
What this person will not be able to do. They cannot grant access to anyone else, and they cannot widen their own access beyond the areas you pick (X-26). Only a full administrator can do that.
Simulated. No access is actually changed. In production this takes effect at once and every later action is attributed to this person by name (X-22, X-24).
