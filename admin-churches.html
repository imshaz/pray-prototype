Churches & leaders

Every participating church in Charleston, its leader, its pray-ers — and which ones have gone quiet.

A participating church is an organisation in the community — not a separate record. You either mark an existing organisation as participating in prayer (ON-1), or add one that isn't in the community yet, which sends an invitation for its leader to claim the account (ON-2). Either way the platform checks for duplicates by name and address, so an organisation already on the platform is never created twice (ON-3, ON-4).

Silent and absent are two different problems. A church that hasn't joined at all (invited, not joined) needs a nudge to its leader to claim the account. A church that joined but has gone quiet needs re-engaging, not re-inviting. The platform keeps them apart so you never treat one as the other (ON-10).
