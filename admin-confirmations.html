Confirmation log

What churches sensed in prayer, gathered in one place. Internal by default — nothing is shared beyond a church unless someone chooses to.

What a confirmation is. During a prayer time, a recorder at each church jots down what was sensed — a scripture, a strategy, an encouragement — on their phone, right after the gathering. It replaces the shared document a church used to pass around (CL-11). The ministry doesn't sit in every huddle, but it can see across all churches from here (CL-12).
