A recorder logs what your congregation senses in prayer for the church — themes, impressions, confirmations — so nothing is lost between gatherings. You appoint the recorder, and you can remove or replace them at any time (CL-1, CL-2).

The role is deliberately narrow. Being a recorder grants only the ability to log and view this church's confirmations — no moderation, no access to other churches, no wider authority in the ministry (CL-3).
